How LatiFi works

For borrowers

  1. 1

    Create your wallet.

    Create a wallet in the app or import one you already have. You write down a recovery phrase and keep it yourself.

  2. 2

    Post your request.

    Choose the amount, rate, term and number of installments. Lenders see your reputation next to the request.

  3. 3

    Receive and repay.

    When a lender funds it, the money arrives in your wallet. Pay each installment on time to build your reputation.

Get the app

For lenders

  1. 1

    Connect your wallet.

    Create or import a wallet and sign in by signing a message. There is no password to remember.

  2. 2

    Review open requests.

    Filter by risk, term and amount, and sort by the applicant's reputation.

  3. 3

    Fund and collect.

    Fund the request you prefer. Installments arrive in your wallet and you follow each one in your portfolio.

Frequently asked questions

How it works

Do I need to know about cryptocurrencies?

No. The app guides you step by step and uses everyday words. You do need a wallet, and the app creates one for you.

Who sets the interest rate?

The borrower proposes it along with the amount and term. A lender decides whether to fund it.

How many installments can I choose?

From 1 to 6 equal installments. One installment means a single payment.

Security

Does LatiFi hold my money?

No. Funds move from one wallet to another through the contract. LatiFi never holds your funds or your private keys.

What if I lose my recovery phrase?

Nobody can recover it for you, not even LatiFi. Store it offline in a safe place when you create your wallet.

Is the money real?

No. This version is an academic demonstration on the Polygon Amoy test network and uses a test token with no real value.

Which phones does it work on?

Android only for now. An iOS version is future work.
