How LatiFi works

For borrowers

  1. 1

    Create your wallet.

    Create a wallet in the app or import one you already have. You write down a recovery phrase and keep it yourself.

  2. 2

    Post your request.

    Choose the amount, rate, term and number of installments. Lenders see your reputation next to the request.

  3. 3

    Receive and repay.

    When a lender funds it, the money arrives in your wallet. Pay each installment on time to build your reputation.

Get the app

For lenders

  1. 1

    Connect your wallet.

    Create or import a wallet and sign in by signing a message. There is no password to remember.

  2. 2

    Review open requests.

    Filter by risk, term and amount, and sort by the applicant's reputation.

  3. 3

    Fund and collect.

    Fund the request you prefer. Installments arrive in your wallet and you follow each one in your portfolio.
